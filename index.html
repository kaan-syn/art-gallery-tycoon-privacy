Privacy Policy — Art Gallery Tycoon

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Art Gallery Tycoon is an offline, single-player game. This page explains what data the app does and does not handle.

What we collect

Nothing. The game itself collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no server of ours: your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your save.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Like all ad networks, Google may process device information (such as the advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data) to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

On iOS, the app asks for tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you still get ads — just less personalized ones. If you purchase Remove Ads Forever, the ads SDK is not initialized at all and no ad-related data is processed.

Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads Forever purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) and validated through RevenueCat, which handles an anonymous app-generated user id and purchase receipts — never your name, email, or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no external links beyond this policy. It is not directed at children under 13; the free version uses advertising as described above.
